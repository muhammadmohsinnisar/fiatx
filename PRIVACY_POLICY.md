# Privacy Policy for FiatX

**Last updated: December 2024**

## Introduction

FiatX ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our currency conversion mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### Information We Do NOT Collect

FiatX is designed with privacy in mind. We do **NOT** collect:
- Personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
- Location data or GPS coordinates
- Device identifiers or advertising IDs
- Usage analytics or tracking data
- Financial information or transaction history
- Contact lists or social media accounts

### Information We May Collect

**Currency Exchange Data**: We retrieve real-time currency exchange rates from public APIs to provide accurate conversion rates. This data is:
- Publicly available information
- Not linked to your personal identity
- Cached locally on your device for performance
- Automatically updated to ensure accuracy

**App Functionality Data**: Minimal technical data required for app operation:
- Currency preferences (stored locally on your device)
- Conversion history (stored locally, never transmitted)
- App crash reports (anonymous, no personal data)

## How We Use Information

The limited data we process is used solely to:
- Provide accurate currency conversion rates
- Cache exchange rates for offline functionality
- Improve app stability and performance
- Ensure the app functions correctly

## Data Storage and Security

### Local Storage
- All your preferences and conversion history are stored locally on your device
- No personal data is transmitted to our servers
- You can clear this data anytime by uninstalling the app

### Security Measures
- All network communications use HTTPS encryption
- No user accounts or login systems
- No cloud storage of personal data
- Regular security updates and patches

## Third-Party Services

### Currency Data Providers
We use the following services to obtain exchange rates:
- **Primary**: @fawazahmed0/currency-api (https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/@fawazahmed0/currency-api@latest/v1/)
- **Fallback**: Currency API (https://latest.currency-api.pages.dev/v1/)

These services provide public exchange rate data and do not receive any personal information from our app.

### No Analytics or Advertising
- We do not use Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, or similar services
- No advertising networks or tracking SDKs
- No social media integration or sharing features

## Your Rights and Choices

### Data Control
- **Access**: All your data is stored locally and accessible to you
- **Deletion**: Uninstall the app to remove all local data
- **Portability**: No data lock-in, as everything is stored locally
- **Correction**: Modify preferences directly in the app

### Opt-Out Options
- **Network Access**: You can use the app offline with cached rates
- **Automatic Updates**: Disable in app settings to prevent rate updates

## Children's Privacy

FiatX is suitable for all ages and does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. The app:
- Contains no social features or communication tools
- Does not request personal information
- Has no advertising or in-app purchases
- Is safe for educational use

## International Users

FiatX is available worldwide and complies with international privacy standards:
- **GDPR (European Union)**: Full compliance with data minimization principles
- **CCPA (California)**: No personal information collection or sale
- **PIPEDA (Canada)**: Adherence to privacy protection principles
